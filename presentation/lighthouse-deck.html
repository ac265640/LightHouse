Step 04 · When they leave, and what they're worth

A snapshot hides time. We put it back with survival analysis.

Before the fix
Risk this quarter87%
Months left52
Verdictcontradiction

Everyone in a quarterly snapshot has already survived to their tenure. Ignoring that makes customers look immortal.

The correction
  • Left truncation: each customer enters the risk set at tenure − 3 months
  • Custom Breslow baseline, because lifelines ignores entry time in its baseline
  • Conditional survival: P(still here in k months | here today)
  • CLV = margin × survival-weighted revenue, 60 months, 10% discount
After the fix
c-index, holdout0.830
Quarter-churn AUC0.897
Agrees with classifierρ 0.95
Implied vs actual churn20.8% / 26.5%

Two independent models, one yes-or-no and one survival, now agree. That's what lets us price every save: value at risk = risk × lifetime value if retained.

Step 05 · What actually works

Offer A "cuts churn by 20 points." Compared fairly, the effect disappears.

Effect on churn, percentage points (negative = fewer customers leave)
−20.4
naive Offer A effect, in points
+1.6
causal Offer A effect, in points
Why the naive number lies
  • Offers are tenure-gated. A only reaches 66+ month customers, who rarely leave anyway.
  • Lighthouse compares receivers with non-receivers inside each window, cross-fitted, with propensity weighting.
  • Every 95% interval crosses zero: no offer works on average. The value is in who receives it.
Every campaign ships with an A/B test size
Cross-fitted doubly-robust learner · logistic propensity clipped to [0.05, 0.95] · LightGBM CATE per customer
